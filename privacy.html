Career Ops — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 September 2026

Career Ops is a personal, non-commercial application operated by its developer. This policy explains how the application handles data when connected to a Google account.

Data accessed

When the developer authorizes Career Ops with a Google account, the application may access, strictly for the developer's own use:

  • Gmail — to send the developer's own daily summary reports.
  • Google Drive — to read and store the developer's own application documents.
  • Google Calendar, Contacts, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Tasks — for the developer's own scheduling and document workflows.

How data is used

  • All data is processed solely to perform the functions described above for the developer's own account.
  • No data is sold, rented, or shared with any third party.
  • No data is used for advertising or profiling.
  • The application has no users other than its developer/operator.

Data storage and security

Documents generated by the application are stored in the developer's own Google Drive. Authorization credentials are stored only on the developer's own machines, are encrypted at rest, and are never printed, logged, or transmitted to third parties.
