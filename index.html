Career Ops

Personal job-application automation — track, score, draft, submit, report.

What Career Ops does

Career Ops is a tool that automates your job-application workflow. You connect it to your own Google account, and it takes over the repetitive parts of applying to jobs:

  • Track — it collects job postings you are interested in into one tracker, so nothing gets lost.
  • Score — every posting is scored for fit against your profile, so you spend time on the right roles.
  • Draft — for each application it prepares a tailored CV and cover letter from your master documents.
  • Submit — it fills standard application forms on job boards and company career sites, applying only to postings above your score threshold.
  • Report — at the end of each day it emails you a summary of what was submitted, with the documents attached.

How it works

  1. Sign in with your own Google account and grant Career Ops the permissions listed below.
  2. Add your master CV, your standard answers, and your job-search criteria.
  3. Career Ops runs on a schedule you control: it scans sources, scores new postings, prepares and submits applications, and emails you the daily report.
  4. You stay in control — every permission can be revoked at any time from your Google account.
