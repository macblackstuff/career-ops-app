Career Ops

Career Ops is a personal productivity application built and operated by its developer for private, non-commercial use. It automates the developer's own job-application workflow: tracking job postings, scoring them for fit, generating tailored application documents, and sending a daily summary report by email.

What it does

  • Reads the developer's own job-application tracking data.
  • Sends automated daily summary reports for the developer's own account.
  • Stores generated application documents (CVs, cover letters) in the developer's own cloud storage.

Accounts

Career Ops accesses only the developer's own Google account, solely to perform the actions above on the developer's behalf. It has no other users, collects no data from third parties, and shares nothing.
